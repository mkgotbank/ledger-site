Entry No. 001
Why it exists

Every business deserves a well-kept book.

For centuries, a business was its ledger — a bound book with ruled lines where every sale, every restock, every cent was written down and added up.

Somewhere along the way that clarity got complicated. Inventory tools grew into sprawling dashboards built for warehouses. Sales records scattered across spreadsheets, terminals and tabs. The simple act of knowing what you have, what you sold, and what you actually made started requiring a manual.

The Ledger brings the book back. It is a free app that keeps your records the way a fine ledger always did — close at hand, in order, and yours. It opens on your phone, works with no internet, and one quick free sign-in makes the book yours.

Good records shouldn’t be a luxury. They should be the default.

What that means in practice

  • Free to use. Every feature on this page, today.
  • Offline. Record a sale in a basement, a market stall, an airplane.
  • A minute to begin. Open the link, sign in free, and start tracking.
  • On your device. By default your data never leaves your phone.

What it keeps

One book for the whole business.

Everything below ships in the app today, free to use.

i.

Stock & restocks

Track every item and every restock. Build packs and bundles from your inventory, and watch quantities update themselves as you sell.

ii.

Sales, expenses & profit

Record sales in seconds, log expenses alongside them, and read your real margin on profit & stats dashboards — not a guess, an entry.

iii.

Invoices

Issue clean invoices from your records, with optional Interac e-Transfer payment instructions included for your Canadian customers.

iv.

An AI advisor, in the margins

A built-in AI business advisor reads your numbers and offers honest counsel. Ask it about your business — or ask it how to use the app itself.

v.

Cloud sync & a team, if you want them

Work solo and offline as much as you like — and when you are ready, your account brings sync across devices, multiple businesses in one app, and team roles by email invite: Owner, Admin, Staff, Viewer. Field-level merge means two devices editing at once never lose a side.

vi.

A full audit log

Like a proper ledger, nothing vanishes quietly. Sales, edits, deletions and restores are all recorded, so you can always trace what changed and when.
